### Hex digits

| Hex | 0–9 | A | B | C | D | E | F |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Decimal | 0–9 | 10 | 11 | 12 | 13 | 14 | 15 |
| Binary | | 1010 | 1011 | 1100 | 1101 | 1110 | 1111 |

### Hex to decimal (4 digits)

`WXYZ` = **W × 4096 + X × 256 + Y × 16 + Z**

| W or X value | × 4096 | × 256 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 4096 | 256 |
| 2 | 8192 | 512 |
| 8 | 32768 | 2048 |
| 10 (A) | 40960 | 2560 |
| 11 (B) | 45056 | 2816 |
| 12 (C) | 49152 | 3072 |
| 13 (D) | 53248 | 3328 |
| 14 (E) | 57344 | 3584 |
| 15 (F) | 61440 | 3840 |

Small values: `0014` = 20, `001C` = 28, `0020` = 32, `002A` = 42, `0035` = 53, `0050` = 80, `01BB` = 443.

### Units

- 1 byte = 8 bits; 1 KB = 1000 bytes in delay sums
- 1 ms = 10⁻³ s, 1 μs = 10⁻⁶ s, 1 ps = 10⁻¹² s
- 1 kbps = 10³ bps, 1 Mbps = 10⁶, 1 Gbps = 10⁹
